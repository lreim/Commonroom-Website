Get Help Now

If you are in immediate danger, feel unsafe, or think you may harm yourself or someone else, please contact emergency services or an urgent professional support service right away.

When CommonRoom isn't enough

CommonRoom is a peer-to-peer platform for conversations and shared experiences between students. It is not a crisis service or a substitute for professional care. Messages and chat requests may also take time or remain unanswered.
